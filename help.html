How I can help

You need a team. Here’s where I fit in.

Four different starting points, one way of working underneath: seeing the bigger picture, holding complexity, finding what’s really in the way, and getting into action together. Find yourself below.

  • The overwhelmed entrepreneur
  • The business owner with scattered systems
  • The organizational leader
  • The AI-curious individual

The overwhelmed entrepreneur

You’ve been dreaming about it long enough. Let’s start building it.

You have a business, a side business, or an idea that matters deeply to you. Maybe you’ve been thinking about it for years. You might be balancing it with parenting, caregiving, or a job. There are notebooks, browser tabs, half-finished documents, and a list of things you keep meaning to do.

  • “I know what I want to create, but I don’t know where to start.”
  • “I keep working in my business instead of on it.”
  • “I don’t need another course. I need help actually doing it.”

What you can hire me to do

  • Turn your vision into an actionable business plan
  • Clarify priorities and decide what matters most
  • Break big projects into manageable steps
  • Spot bottlenecks and competing demands
  • Decide what to drop, delegate, automate, or simplify
  • Build a communications plan or content schedule
  • Create marketing graphics and materials
  • Evaluate tools and technology
  • Find practical uses for AI
  • Project management and hands-on execution

What could you accomplish if you didn’t have to do it all yourself?

What changes: progress you can see, momentum that keeps going, and a growing sense that your vision is actually achievable.

The business owner with scattered systems

Your business has grown. Let’s make sure your systems can keep up.

You’re already running a business, and the systems that keep it running are wearing you out. More than one email account and calendar. Client records in one place, spreadsheets in another, documents everywhere. Subscriptions you forgot you’re paying for. Too much time spent searching, re-entering, and holding it all together from memory.

  • “I can’t find anything when I need it.”
  • “I have three calendars and still miss things.”
  • “Technology is supposed to save me time. Why is it creating more work?”

What you can hire me to do

  • Connect scattered email and calendars
  • Organize, sort, and tag information
  • Build searchable libraries and knowledge bases
  • Evaluate and compare software options
  • Simplify workflows and business processes
  • Improve client scheduling and onboarding
  • Automate appropriate repetitive tasks
  • Develop AI-assisted workflows
  • Cut unneeded apps and subscriptions
  • Document processes so they’re easy to maintain

Are your tools working together, or are you the one holding everything together?

What changes: your information is easier to find, your tools support your work instead of adding to it, and there’s less to carry in your head. I don’t just recommend the fix. I help put it in place.

The organizational leader

Technology only creates value when people can actually use it.

Your organization is bringing in AI tools, new software, or new processes, and you know the technology is only part of the challenge. People may be overwhelmed, skeptical, or quietly sticking with the old workarounds. Technical and business teams may not be speaking the same language. And nobody has time to fix the processes underneath.

  • “We’ve invested in technology, but people aren’t using it.”
  • “Everyone’s talking about AI, but we don’t have a clear strategy.”
  • “We need experienced help without hiring another full-time employee.”

I’ve spent two decades working between technical teams and the people who use what they build, as a project manager, business analyst, and change leader. I’m at home in rooms with tech teams, researchers, clinicians, and leaders, helping them understand each other. I’ve worked across nonprofits, research, healthcare, and small businesses.

What you can hire me to do

  • AI readiness and adoption support
  • Human-centered technology implementation
  • Organizational change management
  • Stakeholder engagement
  • Requirements gathering and business analysis
  • Project planning and implementation support
  • Business process assessment and improvement
  • Training and educational materials
  • Finding adoption barriers and workarounds
  • Cross-functional communication and alignment
  • Technology evaluation
  • Short-term and project-based consulting

Is your AI initiative focused on implementing technology, or on enabling people?

What changes: better-informed decisions, clearer processes and communication, real implementation progress, and better conditions for people to adopt what you’ve invested in. Organizational work is scoped and quoted per engagement.

The AI-curious individual

Let’s take the mystery out of AI.

You’ve heard a lot about AI, but you’re not sure how it works, what it can do, or whether it could help you. Maybe you tried ChatGPT once and wondered what the excitement was about. Maybe you’re skeptical, or worried about privacy. You don’t have to be a technology person to make technology work for you.

  • “Everyone seems to understand AI except me.”
  • “I don’t even know what questions to ask.”
  • “I wish someone could just sit down and show me.”

What you can hire me to do

  • Personal, approachable AI education
  • Demonstrations using your own examples
  • Get started with ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or what fits
  • Write prompts that get useful answers
  • Explore AI for everyday life and business
  • Understand limits, privacy, and responsible use
  • Build confidence through hands-on practice
  • Know when a simpler solution is better

What would you love to learn if you didn’t have to figure it out alone?

What changes: curiosity turns into confidence. You understand what you’re using, how to check it, and what to keep to yourself, and you’ve tried it on something real. Shame-free, at your pace. More on how I use and teach AI →

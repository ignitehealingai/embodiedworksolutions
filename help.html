What we can work on

Real help for real-world problems.

I work where technology, people, and projects meet. Here are some of the places we might begin—not boxes you have to fit yourself into.

01 / AI & technology support

Make the tools work for you.

We’ll find the right level of technology for what you’re trying to do, then make it useful in your actual work. That might mean understanding AI, fixing a frustrating setup, or building a repeatable way of getting things done.

  • Get started with ChatGPT or other AI tools in a practical, confident way
  • Untangle an annoying digital task or technology problem
  • Use AI to organize ideas, draft materials, or simplify recurring work
  • Connect information, documents, and everyday workflows
02 / Business systems & project support

Turn the moving pieces into a plan.

When a project feels too big, a workflow is breaking down, or your business relies on remembering everything yourself, we’ll map what’s happening and make the next steps clearer.

  • Turn an idea into a project plan you can actually follow
  • Map a process and find where things get stuck
  • Set up organizing tools, responsibilities, and handoffs
  • Move a stalled project toward decisions and deliverables

Not sure which category applies? You don’t need to choose one. That’s what our first conversation is for.

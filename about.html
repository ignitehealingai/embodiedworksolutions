About

A real person in your corner.

Hi, I’m SK.

I help people move from idea to solution. You bring an idea, a project that’s grown complicated, or a pile that needs sorting. Together we get clear on the goal, figure out where and how to start, and take the next steps.

That usually looks like one of two things: organizing scattered pieces into a coherent plan, or learning new technology—right now, that’s AI. We start with your own words, exactly where you are.

Most project help starts with your calendar. Ours starts with you. First, I pay attention to what your body is telling us and where you’re getting stuck. I take a whole-person approach, drawing on clinical social work training and years of embodiment work, so I may ask about your self-care, your well-being, and the support you have around you. Then we build the plan around all of it. That’s the “embodied” in Embodied Work Solutions.

What it’s like to work with me

See more clearly. Move forward together.

I hold the threads with you—every project, idea, and loose end—so your mind has room to think. I learn how your brain works and build around it. And when the next step isn’t clear yet, we figure it out together.

When a step feels hard, we get curious about it. Maybe it becomes smaller. Maybe we take it together. Maybe someone else is the right person for it. Every pause tells us something useful.

Learning new technology works the same way: at your pace, in the way you learn best, with plenty of room for questions.

The approach behind the work

Regulation-Centered System Design™

This is the framework underneath everything I do, and the idea at its center is simple: systems work when they align with our nervous systems.

RCSD combines what I know from embodiment and nervous-system work with what I know from systems, project management, and technology.

Every plan, tool, or system we build gets the same test: does it help you stay steady, connected to yourself, and in charge of how you use it? If a tool or app is throwing you off, we find a better option or adjust the one you have until it works for you.

What that means in practice

People first.Tools second.

We choose solutions you can understand and sustain, at a pace your life can hold. When a simpler, non-AI solution fits better, that’s what I’ll recommend. When we use AI, I explain how it works and ask before putting your information into any AI tool.

I think of my work a bit like the mycelium under these mushrooms: connecting people, ideas, and information below the surface, so what’s above ground can grow.

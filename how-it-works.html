How it works

A simple way from stuck to moving.

We don’t start with a complicated assessment or a long sales pitch. We start with what you’re trying to accomplish.

01 / TALK

Tell me what’s happening.

We meet on Zoom for a free 20-minute conversation. You can bring a question, an idea, a tangled situation, or simply the sense that something needs to change.

02 / FIND THE FIT

Choose a first move.

I’ll be direct about whether I can help. If it makes sense, we’ll agree on a focused session, a small project, or a longer partnership.

03 / BUILD

Work on the actual thing.

We untangle the problem, test possibilities, and build something useful. Your needs—not the newest software—shape the approach.

04 / HANDOFF

Make it yours.

We make sure you understand what we’ve created, what to do next, and how to maintain it. No unnecessary complexity or dependency.

A few things you might be wondering

Before we talk.

What if I don’t even know what I need?

That’s completely fine. Tell me what’s frustrating, unfinished, or taking more energy than it should. We can figure out the shape of the problem together.

Do I have to use AI?

No. AI is one possible tool, not the point of the work. The point is to solve the right problem in a way that fits you.

Do I need to prepare for our first call?

No presentation or technical explanation required. You can bring a specific question or just start talking about what’s going on.

How do payment and scheduling work?

The first 20-minute Zoom conversation is free. If we decide to work together, we’ll agree on the scope and cost before any paid work begins. Invoices are handled through Wave.

The next step is small

Bring me the thing you’re stuck on.

You don’t need a perfect explanation, a polished idea, or the right terminology. We’ll start with a conversation and see what makes sense from there.

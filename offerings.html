Offerings

Three ways I can help.

Each one starts with a conversation about where you are right now.

Build the thing

Have an idea you want to build?

A website that introduces your work properly. An app you can picture but haven’t built. A custom tool for one very specific problem—like a shopping list that’s ready when you get to the store, or a math app made for one kid.

We can build it together, or you can hand me an agreed project and get back to what needs you.

You might walk away with

  • A working website, app, or tool
  • Something tested on your real life
  • Help understanding how to use it and keep it going
Let’s bring it to life →

Systems that fit

Need a system that actually fits how you live and work?

Calendars that don’t talk to each other. Information scattered across five apps. A weekly rhythm that only works on good days.

We’ll look at what’s really happening, find the tools that fit, and set them up so they still hold on a hard day.

You might walk away with

  • One place to find what you need
  • Apps and calendars that work together
  • A routine or plan that fits your actual week
Let’s find what fits →

Getting started with AI

Curious about AI and unsure where to start?

You know AI is out there and could probably help you, but you don’t know where to begin. Or you’ve already tried it and have a growing collection of drafts, suggestions, and experiments.

We’ll look at your work and your days together, find the places AI could actually make something easier, and try it side by side. I’ll help you decide which possibilities deserve your time—and check how well they work.

You might walk away with

  • A first workflow you can use right away
  • A clearer sense of where AI helps you—and where it doesn’t
  • A prototype or a clear next step

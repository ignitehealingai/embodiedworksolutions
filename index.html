The approach behind the work

People first.Tools second.

My work is informed by Regulation-Centered System Design™—an approach that takes real human capacity seriously. We’ll choose solutions you can understand and sustain. If we use AI, I’ll explain how and ask before putting your information into an AI tool. And if a simpler, non-AI solution is better, that’s what I’ll recommend.

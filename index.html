How it works

We start where you are.

01

Tell me what’s hard—or what you want to make

A conversation, rough notes, or an unfinished attempt is plenty to begin.

02

Sometimes that’s a tool that already exists. Sometimes it’s a simple system. Sometimes we build something just for you.

03

We test it on your real life—including a hard day—and adjust until it works.

Small projects are welcome. We agree on scope and cost before we start. Written next steps and check-ins that suit you—neurodivergent folks especially welcome.

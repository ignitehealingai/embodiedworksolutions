What makes this different

Embodiment meets systems.

The best systems are built for the body that uses them. A planner, an app, or a workflow works best when it fits your real days—the tired, stressed, and full ones as well as the easy ones. That’s a design question, and it has good answers.

My approach, Regulation-Centered System Design™, combines embodiment with systems thinking, so the plans, tools, and habits we build work with your nervous system.

  • Start in the body.Every session begins grounded, and we pay attention to body signals as we work.
  • Build for real capacity.Plans and tools sized to the time, energy, and attention you actually have.
  • Let the system carry the load.One running list, regular check-ins, small adjustments. The list holds it, so your head is free for the work.
  • Your expertise, my process.You know your subject. I bring the questions, the path, and the right experts when we need them.
